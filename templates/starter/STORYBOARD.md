1080x1920, 30 fps, 60-75 s. Workflow, in this order: storyboard → 3D and animation → script and voice fitted to the shots → assembly.

## World
Where everything stands (metres, +y up, characters face +z). Which set pieces the physics decides the position of.

## Cast
Ben (crash-test hero), narrator ("you", builds things), neighbour (victim of every escalation), grandma (the punchline).

## Physics
Every motion that has to look right, with its formula or model, and which numbers are solved so beats land in their shots.

## Labels
The on-screen words (short, all caps).

## Shot list
W = wide (whole set + action + characters), M = medium, C = close (reactions only, ≤ 1 s). Aim for 45-60 shots of 1-1.5 s.
